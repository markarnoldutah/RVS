This is the whole agreement — one page, meant to be read aloud and signed on a phone or tablet **in the room**, before the location's slug goes live. Do not attach it to an email for later review; if it can't be signed in the meeting, the meeting didn't close. Fill in the bracketed fields, read the pricing and the license paragraph out loud, and get a signature before you leave.

## Pilot Agreement

This Pilot Agreement ("**Agreement**") is between **RV ServiceFlow** ("**RVS**", "**we**") and **[Shop / Business Legal Name]** ("**Customer**", "**you**"), doing business as **[DBA, if different]**, for the location(s) at **[Address]** (each, a "**Location**").

### 1. What you get

RVS provides a hosted intake form, AI-assisted diagnostic questions, and an emailed service packet (readable write-up, photos, printable PDF) for each Location, plus a customer status link. Full description: `rvserviceflow.com` and the current product overview.

### 2. Pilot term and price

- The pilot runs **30 days from the date below**, free, starting the day the first Location's intake slug goes live.
- After 30 days, billing begins automatically at the rate checked below unless either party cancels first — no separate signature required to convert.

| Plan | Rate | Applies when |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ Mobile technician | **$39/mo**, locked for the life of this Agreement | No fixed shop location |
| ☐ Shop, 1–2 locations | **$79/location/mo** | Independent shop |
| ☐ Shop, 3–9 locations | **$69/location/mo** | Small group |
| ☐ Shop, 10+ locations | **$59/location/mo** | Larger group |

Two months free on annual prepay. Fair use: 300 submissions/location/month; extraordinary volume is a conversation, not an automatic overage charge. Price is locked for this Customer at the rate checked above for as long as the Agreement continues, regardless of future list-price changes.

### 3. Cancellation

Either party may cancel with written notice (email is sufficient) effective at the end of the current billing month. No minimum term beyond the free pilot period. No cancellation fee.

### 4. Anonymized data license (X-3)

Customer grants RVS a **perpetual, irrevocable license** to use data submitted through the Location's intake form — service requests, diagnostic categories, and outcomes — in **anonymized, aggregated form**, stripped of any information identifying Customer, its Location(s), its customers, or any individual. RVS will never sell or share Customer's or its customers' identifiable information. This license survives cancellation of this Agreement.

### 5. Confidentiality and data ownership

Customer retains ownership of its raw service data. RVS will not disclose Customer's identifiable data to any third party except as needed to operate the service (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery) or as required by law.

### 6. No warranty; limitation of liability

The service is provided "as is" during the pilot and thereafter. RVS's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the three months preceding any claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

### 7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah.
