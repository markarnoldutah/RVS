# Pitchg to Prospect



### Agenda
- to share what the product is and provide a demo of how it can help you

### Prop
- RV Intake is a mobile intake form for new service requests.  The way it 
works is you send a link tied to your location to the customer by text or email
- When the click the link it opens a simple form, no account setup or login, just and easy guided form through collecting their VIN, attaching their description and photos or videos. 
- After they submit, you'll receive an email with a simple, cleaned one-pager with all their information, including a preliminary assessment of what we think is wrong, possible fixes, and possible parts needed.  
- Meanwhile the customer receives a confirmation email and status link they can use to come back at any time to see status updates you add. 
- So you're getting two things: a structured, triaged report of the issue, and a simple portal to reduce phone tag with the customer. 
- You can also post a QR code on your truck, biz cards, web site to make it easy for customers to go straight the intake form.

### Value 
- Right part on the truck 
- Less phone tag:  "what's the VIN again?  What are we waiting on now? " 
- Big operation look and feel 
- Evidence: written documentation of the issue as submitted
- An intake link in voicemail if your hands are tied up on another job
- Notify customer through the app if you don't service their issue

### User Research
- how many trips last month were the wrong part, or wrong problem, and what does that cost each time? 
- what resonates most in the value prop? 
- what's your primary customer intake channel: text, email, phone? 
- how many service requests per week?
- Reaction to the data clause: "The agreement lets us use service data in anonymized, aggregate form, with your name and your customer names removed.  You still own the raw data, and we never sell or share anything that identifies you or your customers, but we may use the rig data to analyze repair trends etc."
- Do you think $39/mo is reasonable for a one man shop?  A dealer like a Blue Compass would pay more per location.
