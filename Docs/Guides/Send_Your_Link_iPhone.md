# Send your service link — iPhone

> **Draft.** The menu paths here have not yet been confirmed on a real iPhone. Check the
> list at the end before handing this to anyone.

Customers call about a problem with their RV, describe it over the phone, and you write down
what you can. This sets your phone up to send them a link instead — they fill in the details,
add photos of the actual problem, and it arrives as a one-page write-up.

Two things to set up. Ten minutes, once.

**You'll need your link.** It looks like `go.rvintake.com/your-store-name`. If you don't have
it, ask whoever set up your RVS account. Use this exact link — it's how we can tell you later
which of these actually brought jobs in.

---

## 1. Teach your keyboard to type the link

You don't want to type that link a dozen times a day. Give it a shortcut.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **General**, then **Keyboard**, then **Text Replacement**.
3. Tap the **+** in the top right.
4. In **Phrase**, type the whole message you want to send. Something like:

   > Thanks for calling! Tap here to send us the details and a few photos: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

5. In **Shortcut**, type something short you'd never type by accident — `rvlink` works.
6. Tap **Save**.

Now, anywhere you can type, typing `rvlink` and a space turns into the whole message.

Try it in Notes before you use it on a customer.

---

## 2. Set up a reply you can send without answering

When a call comes in and you can't pick up, iPhone can text the caller back for you.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Phone**.
   *(On older iPhones, Phone is in the main Settings list instead of under Apps.)*
2. Tap **Respond with Text**.
3. Tap one of the three messages and replace it with:

   > Sorry I missed you — send us the details here and we'll call you right back: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

4. Tap outside the box to save.

These slots are short. If your message won't fit, cut the first half, not the link.

---

## 3. Using it

**On a call you can't take.** As the phone rings, swipe up on the small **Message** icon
(or tap **Message** if the screen is unlocked) and pick the reply you saved. The call ends
and the text goes out.

**On a call you already took.** After you hang up:

1. Open **Phone**, then **Recents**.
2. Tap the **ⓘ** next to the number you just spoke to.
3. Tap **Message**.
4. Type `rvlink`, then a space. The full message appears.
5. Send.

**Either way, don't retype the link by hand.** A typed link is a job we can't trace back to
the call that produced it.

---

## If something doesn't work

**The shortcut doesn't expand.** You need a space (or punctuation) after it. And check you
typed the shortcut, not the phrase.

**The customer says the link doesn't open.** Have them read it back. If your message got cut
off mid-link, shorten the words before the link.

**The link opens a "location not found" page.** The store name in your link is wrong. Ask
whoever set up your account for the exact link.
