# Send your service link — Pixel and other Android phones

> **Draft.** The menu paths here have not yet been confirmed on a real Pixel. Check the list
> at the end before handing this to anyone.
>
> **Samsung owners: use [the Samsung guide](Send_Your_Link_Samsung.md) instead.** Samsung
> ships its own keyboard and its own phone app, and every menu below is in a different place.

Customers call about a problem with their RV, describe it over the phone, and you write down
what you can. This sets your phone up to send them a link instead — they fill in the details,
add photos of the actual problem, and it arrives as a one-page write-up.

Two things to set up. Ten minutes, once.

**You'll need your link.** It looks like `go.rvintake.com/your-store-name`. If you don't have
it, ask whoever set up your RVS account. Use this exact link — it's how we can tell you later
which of these actually brought jobs in.

---

## 1. Teach your keyboard to type the link

You don't want to type that link a dozen times a day. Give it a shortcut.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **System**, then **Keyboard**, then **On-screen keyboard**, then **Gboard**.
3. Tap **Dictionary**, then **Personal dictionary**, then **English (US)**.
4. Tap the **+** in the top right.
5. In the top box, type the whole message you want to send:

   > Thanks for calling! Tap here to send us the details and a few photos: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

6. In **Shortcut**, type something short you'd never type by accident — `rvlink` works.
7. Tap back to save.

**This one works a little differently from an iPhone.** Android doesn't swap the text in
automatically. Type `rvlink` and the full message appears as a **suggestion in the strip
above the keyboard** — tap the suggestion to insert it.

Try it in Messages before you use it on a customer.

---

## 2. Set up a reply you can send without answering

When a call comes in and you can't pick up, your phone can text the caller back for you.

1. Open the **Phone** app.
2. Tap the **⋮** (three dots) in the top right, then **Settings**.
3. Tap **Quick responses**.
4. Tap one of the messages and replace it with:

   > Sorry I missed you — send us the details here and we'll call you right back: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

5. Tap **OK**.

If you don't see **Quick responses**, your Phone app version doesn't have it. Skip this step
and use section 3's "call you already took" method instead — it works on every phone.

---

## 3. Using it

**On a call you can't take.** As the phone rings, swipe up on the **Reply** or **Message**
icon and pick the reply you saved. The call ends and the text goes out.

**On a call you already took.** After you hang up:

1. Open **Phone**, then **Recents**.
2. Tap the number you just spoke to.
3. Tap **Message**.
4. Type `rvlink` and tap the suggestion that appears above the keyboard.
5. Send.

**Either way, don't retype the link by hand.** A typed link is a job we can't trace back to
the call that produced it.

---

## If something doesn't work

**No suggestion appears.** Personal dictionary entries only show as suggestions, and only
in Gboard. Check that Gboard is the keyboard you're actually using — you'll see its name at
the bottom of the keyboard, or in Settings → System → Keyboard.

**The suggestion shows the shortcut, not the message.** Tap it anyway; it inserts the full
message.

**The customer says the link doesn't open.** Have them read it back. If your message got cut
off mid-link, shorten the words before the link.

**The link opens a "location not found" page.** The store name in your link is wrong. Ask
whoever set up your account for the exact link.
