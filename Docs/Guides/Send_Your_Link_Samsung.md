# Send your service link — Samsung Galaxy

> **Draft.** The menu paths here have not yet been confirmed on a real Galaxy. Check the list
> at the end before handing this to anyone.

Customers call about a problem with their RV, describe it over the phone, and you write down
what you can. This sets your phone up to send them a link instead — they fill in the details,
add photos of the actual problem, and it arrives as a one-page write-up.

Two things to set up. Ten minutes, once.

**You'll need your link.** It looks like `go.rvintake.com/your-store-name`. If you don't have
it, ask whoever set up your RVS account. Use this exact link — it's how we can tell you later
which of these actually brought jobs in.

---

## 1. Teach your keyboard to type the link

You don't want to type that link a dozen times a day. Give it a shortcut.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **General management**, then **Samsung Keyboard settings**.
3. Tap **More typing options**, then **Text shortcuts**.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. In **Shortcut**, type something short you'd never type by accident — `rvlink` works.
6. In **Expanded phrase**, type the whole message you want to send:

   > Thanks for calling! Tap here to send us the details and a few photos: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

7. Tap **Add**.

Now, typing `rvlink` offers the full message above the keyboard — tap it to insert.

Try it in Messages before you use it on a customer.

**If you use Gboard instead of the Samsung keyboard**, this menu won't be there — follow
[the Pixel guide](Send_Your_Link_Android.md) for step 1 and come back here for step 2.

---

## 2. Set up a reply you can send without answering

When a call comes in and you can't pick up, your phone can text the caller back for you.

1. Open the **Phone** app.
2. Tap the **⋮** (three dots) in the top right, then **Settings**.
3. Tap **Quick decline messages**.
4. Tap the **+** and add:

   > Sorry I missed you — send us the details here and we'll call you right back: go.rvintake.com/your-store-name

5. Tap **Save**.

Samsung keeps your existing messages, so add yours rather than replacing one — it'll be in
the list when you need it.

---

## 3. Using it

**On a call you can't take.** As the phone rings, drag up the **Send message** tab at the
bottom of the screen and pick the reply you saved. The call ends and the text goes out.

**On a call you already took.** After you hang up:

1. Open **Phone**, then **Recents**.
2. **Swipe left** on the entry for the number you just spoke to — that opens a message to them.
   *(Swiping right calls them back, so swipe the correct way.)*
3. Type `rvlink` and tap the phrase that appears above the keyboard.
4. Send.

**Either way, don't retype the link by hand.** A typed link is a job we can't trace back to
the call that produced it.

---

## If something doesn't work

**Nothing appears when you type the shortcut.** Text shortcuts only work on the Samsung
keyboard. Check which keyboard you're using — its name shows at the bottom of the keyboard,
or under Settings → General management → Keyboard list and default.

**Swiping does the wrong thing.** Samsung lets you set which direction messages and which
calls, under Phone → ⋮ → Settings → Swipe to call or send messages. Check which way yours
is set.

**The customer says the link doesn't open.** Have them read it back. If your message got cut
off mid-link, shorten the words before the link.

**The link opens a "location not found" page.** The store name in your link is wrong. Ask
whoever set up your account for the exact link.
